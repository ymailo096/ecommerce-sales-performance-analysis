# Tableau Dashboard

The Tableau dashboards provide an interactive view of e-commerce sales
performance, customer behavior, traffic channels, geography, devices,
and product categories.

## Dashboards

### Executive Overview

The Executive Overview provides a high-level view of overall business
performance.

It includes:

- Total Revenue
- Sessions
- Registered Users
- Revenue per Session
- Revenue Trend
- Sessions Trend
- Top 5 Countries by Revenue
- Revenue by Device
- Revenue by Continent

![Executive Overview](./Executive%20Overview.png)

### Sales Performance

The Sales Performance dashboard focuses on customer registration,
engagement, revenue trends, acquisition channels, and product
performance.

It includes:

- Registration Rate
- Email Confirmation Rate
- Revenue per Registered User
- Sessions per Registered User
- Weekly Revenue Trend
- Weekly Sessions Trend
- Revenue by Traffic Channel
- Revenue by Product Category

![Sales Performance](./Sales%20Performance.png)
